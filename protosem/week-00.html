Week 0 at PRICE Protosem: Stepping into the Unknown

Forge Innovation & Ventures • ~3 Min Read

Starting any intensive fellowship brings a sudden shift in pace. Walking into Week 0 of the PRICE Protosem at Forge Innovation & Ventures, I felt a familiar tension between hesitation and curiosity. Moving out of routine comfort zones into a dynamic cohort is rarely seamless, but the first few days quickly turned initial apprehension into momentum.

Building the Foundation

The opening days focused heavily on breaking down silos. Through fast-paced icebreakers and group exercises, the initial awkwardness dissolved into genuine collaboration. Working alongside peers from completely different disciplines highlighted how much clarity comes from simply listening before trying to solve.

Digital Identity & Self-Awareness

  • Professional Presence: The LinkedIn workshop shifted my perspective from merely maintaining a profile to intentionally articulating value and building industry relevance.
  • Personality Assessment: Analyzing personal work styles and strengths provided a grounded look at where I contribute naturally and where I need deliberate practice in a team setting.

Narrative & Problem Solving

  • Storytelling with Zen Pencils: This session broke down how to translate abstract ideas into structured, relatable narratives—an essential skill when pitching solutions to complex problems.
  • The Marshmallow Challenge: Building a freestanding structure under time constraints reinforced a core engineering truth: over-planning without testing fails. Early prototyping, rapid failure, and iterative adjustment beat rigid assumptions every time.

Looking Ahead

Week 0 was less about structured coursework and more about calibrating our mindset for what lies ahead. It set clear expectations around collaboration, accountability, and practical problem-solving.

With the foundational week complete, I am looking forward to diving deep into the core 20-week PRICE Protosem journey, testing hypotheses, and building real-world solutions alongside this cohort.
